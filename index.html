      A quick guide to AP & washing terms
      Fresh AP
      New AP earned from levelling or job advancement. Allocating it does not cost an AP Reset.
      AP Reset notation
      The minus is the stat you remove from; the plus is the stat you add to. For example, −MP +HP moves one AP from MP to HP.
      Fresh vs. Stale HP Wash
      A Fresh HP Wash puts new AP into HP, paired with an AP Reset from MP to your Main Stat. A Stale HP Wash uses an AP Reset directly from MP to HP.
      HP/MP Pool
      AP you have placed into HP or MP. Keep the AP specified in your plan in this pool until the listed washes are complete.
